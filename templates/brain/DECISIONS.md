# Durable Decisions

Record decisions that materially affect future work. Keep superseded decisions for chronology.

## Entry template

```text
## YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title
Status: CURRENT | SUPERSEDED | CONDITIONAL

Decision:
- ...

Reason / evidence:
- ...

Supersedes:
- ... or NONE

Follow-up:
- ...
```

Do not record unselected brainstorming as a decision.
